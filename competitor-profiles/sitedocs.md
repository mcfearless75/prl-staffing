## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: Replace paper-based safety processes with a digital platform that is easy enough for non-technical field workers to actually use.

**Target audience**: Construction companies, contractors, general contractors — from SMEs to multinationals. Strong fit for businesses with dispersed field teams.

**Positioning angle**: Ease-of-adoption first. They lead with "Don't waste another minute with paper" and G2 "Easiest to Use" recognition rather than feature depth.

**Key messaging themes**:
- Simplicity for field workers (offline mode, voice dictation, mobile-first)
- Proven ROI — customer case study cites "22.5% decrease in injury rates" and "over $100k first-year savings"
- SOC2 certified security
- Real-time visibility across all job sites
- All-inclusive (no per-form or per-seat restrictions on core plan)

## Product & Features

### Core capabilities

| Category | Features |
|----------|----------|
| **Forms & Docs** | Custom form builder, PDF library, digital signatures with GPS + timestamp |
| **Workers** | Worker profiles, certifications tracking, expiry alerts, orientations |
| **Contractors** | Contractor participation + compliance tracking |
| **Safety** | Incident management, hazard management, corrective actions, near-miss reporting |
| **Inspections** | Equipment + vehicle inspection workflows |
| **Monitoring** | Safety inbox, real-time dashboard (Safety Monitor), AI-powered analytics |
| **Mobile** | Offline mode with auto-sync, voice dictation, photo annotation, in-app chat |
| **Integrations** | Zapier, Dropbox, Google Drive, Microsoft 365, Asana, public API (CRUD) |

### Notable differentiators
- **Offline-first mobile app** — forms saved locally and auto-upload when reconnected (critical for remote sites)
- **No worker email required** — authentication without email for field operatives
- **All-inclusive logins** — unlimited worker accounts, no per-seat fee at field level
- **G2 "Highest Adoption"** — signals genuine field uptake, not shelf-ware

### Weaknesses (from reviews)
- No free trial — high friction for procurement
- Post-submission editing is limited
- Equipment setup process is cumbersome
- Graphics-heavy reports strain the UI
- No email-less auth can be a pain point; workers without email still need workarounds

## Key Takeaways

1. **SiteDocs wins on the field.** Their mobile-offline app is purpose-built for construction sites. PRISM isn't trying to do this — it's an office/management portal.

2. **PRISM wins on QMS depth.** ISO 9001, management reviews, risk register — SiteDocs has none of this. Any PRL client that needs ISO compliance can't use SiteDocs alone.

3. **Pricing is the sharpest contrast.** SiteDocs costs real money ($1,500–$40k+/year). PRISM is already built and internally hosted — zero marginal cost per user.

4. **They're not direct competitors — they're complementary.** SiteDocs targets field safety; PRISM covers workforce compliance and quality management. A large contractor could plausibly run both.

5. **If PRL ever productises PRISM**, SiteDocs is the benchmark to beat on ease-of-use and mobile UX. Their G2 "Easiest to Use" award is earned — their onboarding and field adoption is the thing to study.

## Opportunities for PRISM (if commercialised)

- **ISO 9001 angle** — SiteDocs has no QMS module. PRISM's QMS + safety combo would be a genuine gap-fill for construction firms chasing ISO certification.
- **CIS/Labour compliance niche** — SiteDocs doesn't touch payroll, CIS, or RTW. PRISM's labour-agency-specific features are unmatched.
- **UK market positioning** — SiteDocs is Canada-originated. A UK-built, UK-law-compliant alternative with GDPR-native data handling is a credible differentiator.
- **Pricing transparency** — SiteDocs hides pricing. A clear, published price would be a genuine conversion advantage.
