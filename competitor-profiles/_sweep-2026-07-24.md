## 2. The wider field — who we'd missed

**Direct Requidex substitutes (agency back-office / pay & bill):**
- **RSM InTime** (Bullhorn) — the heavyweight: 850+ agencies, 6m timesheets/yr, native CIS + deemed payroll, credit notes, AWR, bulk retro-rate adjustments. Most complete like-for-like.
- **Timesheet Portal** — Requidex's price segment, published per-contractor pricing, self-billing, holiday accrual, AWR, Sage.
- **ETZ** — back-office spine with the best approval-friction story (passwordless email-link approval, paper/photo timesheet ingestion).
- Also mapped: Access Pay & Bill / FastTrack360, Merit, Zeel (NuEvo), Vincere Pay & Bill, Finity, Primo Time, Flo (software + invoice finance bundle).

**Construction-native wildcard:** **Engage** (engagetech.com) — QR/geofence site clock-in, CIS workers, cost-code allocation per hour, AI RTW verification. Positioned as a hirer supply-chain platform rather than an agency back office, but it is the most construction-native product in the sweep.

**Compliance / site-access field (feature inspiration, not direct competitors):** Biosite, MSite, Causeway Donseed, innDex, DataScope, Boxcore, GoContractor, Skillko (HSI), HandsHQ. Pattern across all of them: verification against authoritative databases (CSCS/CITB, IDVT) and compliance wired to consequences (site entry, clock-in) rather than reports.

**IDVT / RTW providers (integration candidates, per-check pricing):** Credas, TrustID, uComply. Key fact: share codes only exist for visa holders. British/Irish citizens need IDVT via a certified IDSP or manual document checks, so PRISM's share-code flow cannot give statutory-excuse-grade RTW for most of the workforce.

**De-prioritised after checking:** Eploy, JobAdder, Colleague (front-office, no pay & bill), Chronotek (US), Eque2 (ERP), CHAS/Veriforce (accreditation scheme, not software; relevant as a requirement on PRL), SafetyCulture (checklist tool), OneAdvanced Tempest (appears retired).

## 4. Non-build actions surfaced

1. **Apply for CSCS Smart Check IT Partner status** (cscs.uk.com) — prerequisite for N4; there are 36 approved partners including Requidex. Application is a commercial/admin step Paul owns.
2. **IDSP per-check accounts** (Credas or TrustID) — pay-as-you-go pricing exists; needed before N11.
3. **CHAS/Common Assessment Standard** — not software; worth checking PRL's own accreditation posture since labour-only subcontractors count as employees for headcount.

## 5. Mistakes to avoid (uniform across rival reviews)
Support responsiveness is complaint #1 for every incumbent (Access worst). Dated UI and weak reporting are #2. Configurability locked behind paid services breeds resentment. PRISM's advantage as an internal tool is exactly these: keep it.

## 6. Suggested Sprint D shape (for discussion, not started)

Blend the existing queue with the new finds where they share plumbing:

- **D1 "Money loop":** N2 invoice payment status + row 16 import payments + N10 redeployment report + row 15 quick-reports hub (the hub gives the reports a home).
- **D2 "Worker clock":** row 6 AWR 12-week tracker + N3 holiday accrual/balance (same per-worker timesheet-derived computation) + row 13 absence status.
- **D3 "Enforcement":** N1 bulk timesheet chase + N6 compliance-gated assignments + N16 reconciliation check (all reuse existing patterns).
- Hold N4 (Smart Check) and N11 (IDVT) until partner/IDSP applications are in flight; hold row 12 credit notes for D1 or D2 depending on load.
